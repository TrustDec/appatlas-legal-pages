English

Information AppAtlas handles

AppAtlas does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives your searches. When you look up an app, your search term, App Store ID, bundle ID, and selected storefront may be sent directly from your device to Apple’s public App Store and iTunes lookup services to return the result. Apple may receive the request and standard connection information and handles it under Apple’s Privacy Policy. The AppAtlas developer does not receive or store these lookup requests.

Information stored on your device

Favorites, recent lookup history and snapshots, pinned storefronts, selected target and comparison storefronts, language preference, and the optional local monitoring setting are stored on your device. They are not synced to an AppAtlas server. You can remove favorites, clear lookup history in the app, or delete AppAtlas to remove its app data from your device.

Notifications and monitoring

If you enable local monitoring, AppAtlas may request notification permission and use iOS background refresh to check selected public storefront information. Requests go directly to Apple. Monitoring is optional; iOS may delay or skip background refresh. Notifications are local to your device. AppAtlas does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Children and changes

AppAtlas does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the app’s information practices change. The effective date above will be revised when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email kayprivacy@hotmail.com and include “AppAtlas” in the subject.

简体中文

AppAtlas 处理的信息

AppAtlas 无需注册账号，也没有接收查询内容的自有服务器。查询应用时，搜索词、App Store ID、Bundle ID 和所选商店地区可能会由设备直接发送给 Apple 的公开 App Store / iTunes 查询服务，以返回结果。Apple 可能收到这些请求及标准连接信息，并按照其隐私政策处理。AppAtlas 开发者不会收到或保存这些查询请求。

保存在设备上的信息

关注应用、最近查询和历史快照、置顶地区、所选目标地区与对比地区、语言偏好，以及可选的本地监控设置保存在你的设备上，不会同步到 AppAtlas 服务器。你可以在应用中取消关注、清除查询历史，或删除 AppAtlas 以移除设备上的应用数据。

通知与监控

启用本地监控后，AppAtlas 可能请求通知权限，并使用 iOS 后台刷新检查所选地区的公开商店信息。请求会直接发送给 Apple。监控为可选功能；iOS 可能延迟或跳过后台刷新。通知仅在本机生成。AppAtlas 不使用广告、分析或跟踪 SDK。

儿童与政策更新

AppAtlas 不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如果应用的信息处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策和上方生效日期。
