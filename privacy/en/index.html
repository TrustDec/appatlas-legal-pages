When you check an App Store listing, your device sends the search term or app identifier and selected storefront directly to Apple's public lookup service. Google Play searches and listing requests are processed by our API Worker and may include the app or package identifier, search term, selected country, and interface language. The Worker requests public Google Play listing information; store responses and request routing can vary by region.

When cloud report sync is used, AppAtlas sends a random installation token stored in iCloud Key-Value Store and App Store and Google Play report snapshots to our Cloudflare Worker and D1 database. A snapshot may contain the app name and identifier, checked country codes, public listing status, ratings and rating counts, price, chart ranks, and recent review counts. Google Play snapshots also record the selected market and review language; reviewer country is not verified. The service keeps up to the 100 newest reports for an installation. Use Clear History in the app to remove report snapshots. Favorites, pinned storefronts, and preferences remain on your device. Email verification and email monitoring are not enabled at this time, and the app does not submit an email address to this service.

Cloudflare processes requests to provide the API. Apple and Google receive requests made to their respective public store services. AppAtlas does not request Apple or Google account credentials and does not use advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs. To ask us to delete associated cloud report data, contact kayprivacy@hotmail.com. Effective date: September 29, 2026.

SupportAppAtlas is an independent app and is not affiliated with Apple.
