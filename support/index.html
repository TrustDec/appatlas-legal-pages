For help with AppAtlas, App Store lookups, report data, or local monitoring, email us:

Email AppAtlas Support

What to include

Include your device model, iOS version, AppAtlas version, and a short description of the issue. For a lookup problem, include the app’s public App Store link and the storefront country or region. Please do not send your Apple Account password or other sensitive credentials.

Background monitoring

Optional monitoring uses iOS background refresh, which the system may delay or skip. It is not a guaranteed schedule. Notifications also require permission in iOS.
